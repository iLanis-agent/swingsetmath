Swing set math

A swing set sketched on the lawn ends up a third swing squeezed onto a two-swing beam, a fence sitting in the swing path, or a chain order an arm short. Swing set math turns the beam and a labeled bay into the swing count, turns the hang height and a labeled ratio into the clearance in front and behind, and turns the bar and the seat into the chain to order. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Swing plan - the beam at a labeled bay becomes the swing count and the room left at the far end.
  • Clearance plan - the hang height at a labeled ratio becomes the clearance asked in front and behind.
  • Chain plan - the bar minus the seat becomes the chain a side and in all.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 120 cm swing bay, a 2x clearance ratio, a 40 cm seat height - all illustrative defaults you can change. Real swing sets follow the manufacturer's spacing, anchoring and fall-surface rules, and the yard's real obstacles. This is planning arithmetic, not an installation design or a safety rating: measure the real yard and let the manufacturer instructions govern.
